# Code of Conduct

We expect contributors and maintainers to be respectful, inclusive, and
professional.

Please:

- assume good intent
- keep feedback specific and constructive
- avoid harassment, personal attacks, or exclusionary behavior
- help new contributors succeed

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, abusive language,
and repeated disruption of constructive discussion.

Project maintainers may remove comments, lock issues, or block accounts when
necessary to protect the community.
